# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite + TypeScript is preferred, with the final technical choice open to the selected design's needs. Use CSS for simple interactions, Motion for React for component/state transitions, and GSAP + ScrollTrigger only for justified cinematic scroll or pinned storytelling. Technology must serve the experience. Hosting is undecided.

## Users

Potential architecture clients, residential and commercial project owners, real estate developers, investors, and visitors interested in architecture and design. Visitors should perceive the studio's design approach, explore projects, understand the studio, and find a clear route to contact.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio-led website for a premium, contemporary, creative architecture studio. This is a concept project for the DAB Labs portfolio, made to the standard of a real architecture practice's website. It is not a commissioned client project. Architecture and the studio's thinking lead the experience; the website must not feel like a sales landing page.

## Positioning

The intended distinction is a coherent, spatial, editorial digital experience in which architectural photography becomes part of the interface. Any studio-specific philosophy, geographic base, project history, credentials, or commercial distinction remains undecided.

## Operating Context

Visitors discover selected work, explore project galleries and details, learn about the studio's approach and services, and reach contact information. Desktop and mobile require deliberate composition. The eventual site should be suitable for production publishing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Candidate content includes an original entrance, selected projects, galleries, studio/approach, architectural philosophy, services, project details, contact, and a closing/footer. Page and section architecture is deliberately open until creative direction is selected.

Research and creative-direction exploration are complete. The user selected Direction D and approved its homepage flow and design system. Authorized implementation is PHASE 1 only: React/Vite/TypeScript foundation, global typography/tokens, responsive hero, ARAL-to-header transition, main photo mask/crop expansion, and vertical detail spatial depth. Stop after this phase and its real-browser QA. Later homepage sections require a new user approval.

During implementation, DAB Motion and Emil animation skills may be used. After implementation, use Playwright for real-browser desktop/mobile visual QA, inspect screenshots, correct material design defects, and retest.

## Brand Commitments

Premium, contemporary architecture with strong art direction, typography, photography, whitespace, architectural grids, controlled asymmetry, and restrained, memorable motion. Editorial architecture publication sensibility; photographs should participate in the composition. Avoid repetitive card grids and generic AI website aesthetics. Keep one coherent studio identity throughout.

The studio name, logo, palette, fonts, geographic identity, and site language are undecided. An original working name may be proposed during design; it is not an approved brand or a verified available name.

The user confirmed the generated imagery's art direction: warm natural light, pale stone, timber, olive trees, and a calm luxurious architectural atmosphere. Preserve this visual material vocabulary. Direction A was rejected as a candidate for implementation; retain its comp only for comparison. Direction D was selected as the main creative direction. Preserve its monumental ARAL typography, layering with architectural photography, wide main photograph and narrow vertical detail, editorial/spatial composition, and strong yet calm premium character. The design system and interaction blueprint are approved. Implement the actual browser behavior rather than reproducing the static mockup pixel for pixel; authorization remains limited to PHASE 1.

## Evidence on Hand

No approved logo, studio name, project photographs, or project records exist. High-quality sourced or generated concept imagery is permitted. Concept projects and generated imagery must not imply real built commissions. Do not invent clients, awards, testimonials, founding dates, locations, or performance claims as real evidence. Reference-site images are research material, not licensed production assets.

## Product Principles

- Let architecture and project exploration carry the site's identity.
- Establish one coherent creative direction before implementation.
- Use layout and motion to express spatial relationships and support navigation.
- Recompose deliberately for mobile rather than shrink desktop.
- Preserve the distinction between concept content and verified practice facts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve readability, keyboard access, clear navigation, and accessible controls. Honor prefers-reduced-motion with a usable, content-complete experience. Specific compliance level is not yet confirmed.
